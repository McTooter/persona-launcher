Written by a fan with opinions. Not generated, not neutral.

1
Play Persona 5 Royal first. Full stop.

Best onboarding in the series, 120 hours, fixes every flaw of vanilla P5. If you bounce off the first palace, the series isn't for you.

2
Want grief? Persona 3 Reload.

Memento mori isn't a slogan, it's the thesis. The ending will sit in your chest for weeks. Theurgy battles slap.

3
Want warmth? Persona 4 Golden.

Inaba fog, road-trip energy, the funniest cast. Least stylish, most lovable. Adachi did nothing wrong (he did everything wrong).

4
Short fuse? Strikers, then Tactica.

Strikers is the canon action sequel (55h). Tactica is a 30h tactics dessert. Dancing games are victory laps.

5

P5R → P3R → P4G → Strikers → Arena Ultimax → Q/Q2 → P1/P2 retro dig
